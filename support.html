How Headroom works

Add a paycheck, bills, and loans. Headroom shows what’s left until payday after mins and bills, minus spends this window. ClearDate estimates when the last debt clears; Net Worth is assets minus loans. All data stays on your iPhone.

Get help

Email mrkrphl@gmail.com for Headroom math questions, Face ID lock, share/paste/photo capture, or App Store purchase issues. Apple handles refunds for App Store purchases.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove local Headroom data from that device. There is no cloud copy to erase.
